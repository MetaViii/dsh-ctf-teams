# v0.1.27 — panel cleanup, and a writeup that reads like a CTF writeup

## 清理战队：面板里就能删

Solved teams used to pile up under `.ctf-teams/` with no way to reclaim the
space from the app. The 解题面板 now owns that:

- **删除战队** (a live or staged team, captain session): a confirm dialog states
  the team id, the workspace path and exactly what will be removed —
  `12 个文件 · 4.0 KB` — and offers two endings: **移到归档** (the record stays
  reviewable under 「已归档」) or **彻底删除** (the directory goes away and the
  space is freed).
- **清空归档 (N)** (the archived roster): permanently removes every archived
  team at once.
- An **unsolved** team says what gets lost before the click; a solved one says
  that the writeup and artifacts live in the workspace and are untouched.
- The response reports the bytes actually freed (`已彻底删除战队，释放 44.0 KB`).

These two actions are the first that are **not** user turns: deleting files does
not need the captain, so the host performs them directly under the team lock.
They stay gated the same way — captain session for a team, a live session for
the archive roster — and `fileCount` / `diskBytes` are now part of the snapshot
so the panel shows real numbers instead of a guess.

## writeup: 按 CTF 选手的写法

The writeup contract moved into one module, `src/writeup.ts`, shared by the
captain protocol, the panel's 写 WRITEUP action and the completion criterion.
The deliverable is now a normal CTF solve record:

```
# <题目名> — <分类>
## 题目信息     平台/分类/分值、附件、远程地址
## 侦察         真正跑过的命令与真实输出
## 漏洞分析     漏洞点、成因、触发条件、关键代码
## 利用过程     从侦察到 flag 的完整步骤，payload 原始形式 + 编码形式、原始 HTTP 报文
## flag         平台返回的原始响应与 flag 原文
## 参考         公开资料/CVE/别人的思路（没有就删掉）
```

Deliberately gone: any mention of the solving tooling, the agent, the model or
the platform framework, and the 修复建议 / 缓解措施 section — a CTF writeup
documents how the box was popped, it is not a bug report. The rules also say
the content must be what actually ran (no invented or polished output), every
command copy-pasteable, and updates should extend rather than rewrite.

The review-report export keeps its own (internal) shape; its footer no longer
mentions the plugin by name.

## Gates

`verify:dashboard` grew to 48 checks (archive/purge behaviour on real temp
files, authority refusals, the archived roster's size, the writeup prompt's
sections and forbidden words) and `verify:client` now clicks through the cleanup
flow: the dialog appears with the size, 移到归档 posts `mode: archive`, 彻底删除
posts `mode: purge`, and the archived roster posts `purge-archive`.
